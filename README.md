# 📊 HR Analytics

## Executive Workforce & People Analytics

An end-to-end **HR Analytics project** that analyzes employee data to understand workforce structure, attrition, recruitment, performance, engagement, absenteeism, and compliance.

The project transforms raw HR data into **KPIs, visualizations, dashboards, and actionable business insights** using Python and Excel.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to help HR and business stakeholders understand:

* Workforce size and workforce planning
* Employee attrition and retention
* Recruitment performance
* Employee performance
* Compensation and pay parity
* Employee engagement
* Absenteeism and attendance
* Compliance and training
* Department-level HR performance

### Analytical Process

```text
Business Problem
       ↓
Data Understanding
       ↓
Data Cleaning & Preparation
       ↓
KPI & Metric Calculation
       ↓
Data Analysis
       ↓
Visualization
       ↓
Business Insights
       ↓
HR Decision Support
```
